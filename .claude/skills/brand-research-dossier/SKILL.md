# brand-research-dossier

## Purpose
Reusable, sanitized operating procedure for brand research dossier. This public version contains no client or owner records.

## Workflow
1. Load the approved brief and known brand facts.
2. Research market, competitors, audience signals, and visual conventions.
3. Separate sourced facts from recommendations.
4. Deliver an internal dossier; do not publish it.

## Calls and chain
Calls or hands off to: `reference-capture, identify-brand-identity`.

## Dependencies
`web research, optional browser automation`

## Adapter note
Use your preferred search and browser-capture tools.

## Safety
- Read before writing.
- Keep credentials in environment variables.
- Keep client, health, financial, and state data outside this repository.
- External messages, publishing, scheduling, CRM writes, and financial actions require explicit human approval.
